# APEX Website — Project Background & Creative Brief

> **Purpose of this file:** Give Claude Code the business context, approved positioning, website scope, content priorities, design expectations, and guardrails for developing the APEX website. This is a background reference, **not** an instruction to generate the entire website in one pass.
>
> **Status:** Working brief based on project discussions, October 2026. Verify public claims, permissions, service availability, and final copy before launch.

## 1. What is APEX?

**APEX (Alternative Protein Excellence)** is a specialised infrastructure and ecosystem platform in India for research, development, validation, pilot-scale work, and scale-up/manufacturing support in alternative and novel proteins.

APEX is **run and managed by ClearMeat in partnership with NIFTEM, under the Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI) ecosystem**. The website should describe this relationship carefully; do not imply that APEX is itself a government department or that MoFPI directly operates it.

### Approved positioning

> **If you want to build, develop, scale or manufacture alternative protein in India, APEX is the infrastructure platform you plug into.**

### Central idea

APEX is **not merely a laboratory, accelerator, or incubator**. It connects specialised research infrastructure, technical capabilities, pilot development, regulatory/market-entry support, and an industry ecosystem. The website should feel like a **platform people plug into**, rather than a generic biotech brochure.

### Important relationship to ClearMeat

ClearMeat is the operating organisation and has its own website. The APEX website should stand on its own as a neutral, collaborative platform welcoming startups, corporations, international entrants, and institutions. Avoid turning APEX into a ClearMeat product catalogue. ClearMeat should receive appropriate operator credit, while APEX remains the primary brand.

## 2. Why the website exists

The current website is **https://apex.clearmeat.org**. The new site is intended to replace it **only after explicit approval**.

The website must quickly answer:

1. What is APEX and why does it exist?
2. Who can use it?
3. How can APEX help me build, develop, scale, or enter India?
4. What technology pathways does it support?
5. Why should I trust this ecosystem?
6. How do I contact APEX about my specific needs?

**Primary conversion:** Qualified conversations initiated by email, WhatsApp, or a short structured enquiry form. The website does not need to expose pricing or automate purchasing/booking.

## 3. Four audiences

| Audience | Need | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Startups | Specialised research space and shared equipment without building a facility | **Build your product, not your facility.** |
| Corporates | Outsourced/extended alternative-protein R&D and pilot work | **Extend your R&D capability.** |
| Global companies | India localisation, regulatory navigation, development and manufacturing pathways | **Build and scale in India without starting from zero.** |
| Government and research institutions | Collaboration and translation of research toward industrial applications | **Move research towards industrial application.** |

Visitors should find their path within seconds, without navigating a large website.

## 4. Commercial offering — what APEX can support

Present these as elegant, understandable service pathways, not a long price list:

- **Access — Pay-per-seat research access:** Research seat, shared equipment usage, and utilities. **Ingredients/consumables are charged separately**; do not imply they are included.
- **Develop — Contract R&D:** Project-based research and development, product/process work, prototyping, and validation as scoped with the client.
- **Scale — Pilot production and scale-up/manufacturing support:** Use the phrase **“scale-up and manufacturing support”**, rather than claiming unrestricted commercial-scale CDMO manufacturing.
- **Enter India — Market-entry and regulatory support:** Help international companies navigate localisation, relevant ecosystem connections, and regulatory pathways.
- **Learn — Training:** Technical training, workshops, and capability-building engagements.
- **Specialised inputs — ClearX9:** ClearMeat's animal-free cell-culture media may be available for samples/research requirements. Mention discreetly and link to ClearMeat for product details.

**Do not publish** seat pricing, minimum engagement durations, detailed commercial conditions, equipment inventory, facility square footage, seat counts, bioreactor capacity, production output, or unsupported certifications.

## 5. Technology coverage

APEX should visibly support three complementary alternative-protein pathways:

1. **Cellular / cultivated proteins** — cell culture, media, process development, and related research.
2. **Fermentation** — relevant fermentation research and process-development pathways.
3. **Plant-based proteins** — formulation, texturisation, processing, and product development.

Show them as connected pathways supported by a common infrastructure ecosystem, not three disconnected business units. Do not claim specific equipment or service capacity unless verified.

## 6. Approved website scope

**Keep the website compact: two principal pages, plus an interactive contact/enquiry experience.** Avoid creating six or more separate marketing pages.

### Page 1 — Home / APEX platform

Suggested section order (can be refined for narrative flow):

1. **Hero:** APEX brand, platform proposition, cinematic but clean visual, clear CTAs.
2. **What APEX is:** Connected journey from idea/research to development, validation, scale, manufacturing, and market.
3. **Who it serves:** Four audience paths with interactive cards.
4. **How APEX supports you:** Access, Develop, Scale, Enter India, Learn; each offers a way to enquire.
5. **Technology pathways:** Cellular/cultivated, fermentation, plant-based.
6. **Specialised inputs:** Subtle ClearX9 mention.
7. **Infrastructure:** Visual, capability-led explorer **within the homepage**, not a separate page.
8. **Why APEX:** Specialisation, integration, India-connected ecosystem; avoid repeating the service list.
9. **APEX ecosystem:** Institutional context and wider collaborators, designed to accommodate additional organisations.
10. **Updates/media strip:** A small curated section with links to LinkedIn and Instagram; avoid an empty blog or fragile embedded social feed.
11. **Final conversion:** “What are you trying to build?” with service-led contact options.

### Page 2 — Work With APEX

One page with four audience tabs or panels:

- Startups
- Corporate R&D
- Global companies / India entry
- Government and research institutions

Each view should have a concise proposition, relevant APEX services, a simple engagement journey, and a relevant CTA. A click from an audience card on the homepage should open this page with the correct audience preselected.

### Contact / enquiry experience

Prefer an elegant overlay, drawer, or guided form instead of a conventional third content page. Entry points can include “Connect with APEX,” “Access APEX,” “Discuss an R&D Project,” “Explore Scale-up,” and “Enter India.”

Start with audience/service selection, then ask a few **specific, relevant questions**. Capture name, organisation, email, optional WhatsApp contact, project need, and relevant stage/timing. Final questions and form backend are **not yet approved** and should be reviewed before implementation.

Offer email and WhatsApp contact. A bot may later answer enquiries; **do not claim a bot is already integrated**.

## 7. Ecosystem and institutional presentation

Use **“APEX Ecosystem”** rather than describing every organisation as a “Strategic Partner.”

- Institutional context: **ClearMeat, NIFTEM, MoFPI ecosystem**.
- Wider ecosystem organisations identified for possible display: **Bühler, ProVeg, BRINC, BITS BioCyTiH, Nestlé**.
- Leave elegant room for additional organisations (“And growing”).

**Critical:** Verify each organisation's relationship, permission to use its logo, and acceptable wording before publishing. Do not imply that all organisations have signed the same type of agreement, or that inclusion constitutes endorsement. Government logo usage requires appropriate permission.

## 8. Visual and UX ambition

The user rejected a basic, familiar biotech landing-page look. A subsequent **cinematic, editorial, more immersive mockup** was better received. The final site should take that more ambitious direction further, while keeping the structure clear and the interactions useful.

### Desired experience

- Premium, confident, scientific, architectural, and industrial.
- A **connected ecosystem/platform** rather than a standard corporate brochure.
- Cinematic hero with refined typography and strong visual hierarchy.
- Scroll-driven storytelling, subtle pathway activation, meaningful microinteractions.
- Interactive audience cards, services, and capability/facility explorer.
- Balanced dark and light sections, restrained but distinctive colour, beautiful imagery.
- Clear navigation, fast loading, excellent mobile layout, accessible interactions.

### Avoid

- Generic biotech stock-template aesthetic, excessive rectangular card grids, gratuitous DNA helices, floating molecules, neon gradients, or sci-fi clutter.
- Fake facility imagery presented as real APEX photography.
- Overloaded menus, excessive copy, six-plus separate pages, lengthy equipment tables.
- Revenue, financials, market-size counters, facility metrics, or case-study sections at this stage.
- Unsupported claims of commercial manufacturing capacity or guaranteed regulatory outcomes.

### Imagery

Actual facility photos are **not currently available**. Use high-quality **properly licensed** third-party imagery or clearly illustrative visual concepts. Keep imagery modular so it can be replaced with original facility photography later. Do not scrape and reuse arbitrary internet photos without usage rights.

## 9. Navigation and interaction

A compact sticky navigation could use:

**APEX | How It Works | Capabilities | Infrastructure | Ecosystem | Updates | Work With APEX | Connect**

Most items should anchor-scroll to homepage sections. “Work With APEX” opens page two. “Connect” launches the enquiry experience. On mobile, prioritise a clean accessible menu.

A small persistent **Talk to APEX** action can offer enquiry, email, or WhatsApp, without obstructing mobile content.

## 10. Suggested implementation stack

- React + Vite + TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Motion for React for purposeful animation
- Lucide icons
- GitHub for version control
- Netlify for preview and eventual hosting

Keep this a fast, static-first website with a minimal backend. Forms may use a suitable hosted form or serverless service after the workflow is agreed. Avoid introducing a CMS or database without a clear requirement.

## 11. Workflow for Claude Code

1. Read this background brief fully.
2. Audit the repository and propose a simple component structure.
3. Produce a design-system proposal and homepage wireframe/content hierarchy.
4. Implement **hero + navigation first**, including responsive behaviour.
5. Show a working preview and wait for design feedback.
6. Build remaining sections incrementally, then the Work With APEX page.
7. Implement enquiry functionality only after form questions, contact destinations, privacy handling, and backend are approved.
8. Test mobile responsiveness, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, image licences, SEO, and performance.
9. Deploy to a staging/preview URL; **never overwrite https://apex.clearmeat.org without explicit approval**.

## 12. Open decisions before production

- Approved final APEX logo assets, typography, exact colour palette and imagery references.
- Verified wording for NIFTEM/MoFPI institutional relationship and logo permissions.
- Confirmed status and logo permissions for each ecosystem organisation.
- Contact email, WhatsApp number, social media URLs, and bot integration details.
- Final enquiry questions, data destination, privacy notice and spam protection.
- Confirmed scope/availability of individual services and ClearX9 samples.

## 13. Success criteria

A first-time visitor should understand APEX in under a minute, identify their relevant pathway, perceive a credible and differentiated alternative-protein ecosystem, and find an effortless way to contact the team. The site should be visually memorable **without sacrificing clarity, accuracy, accessibility or speed**.

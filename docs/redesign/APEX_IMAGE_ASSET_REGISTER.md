## Download status: blocked

This build environment's network policy refuses connections to `commons.wikimedia.org`, `upload.wikimedia.org`, `unsplash.com`, `images.unsplash.com`, `pexels.com` and `images.pexels.com` (proxy answers 403). **No third-party image has been downloaded or placed in the repo.** Slots without an authentic image show a labelled "Photo pending" panel, not a stand-in picture.

Candidate pages below were read through a separate web-reading tool, which could open Unsplash and Pexels pages (so their licence is marked _verified on page_) but not Wikimedia Commons (marked _unverified_). To finish this step, either allow those hosts in the project's cloud environment network settings, or download the chosen files yourself and attach them to the thread.

## MoFPI: withheld

- No logo or emblem is shown. MoFPI appears only as text: "NIFTEM-Kundli is an institution under India's Ministry of Food Processing Industries. APEX is not operated by MoFPI."
- MoFPI's website policy says its content "may not be reproduced partially or fully, without due permission" and that prior permission is needed even for hyperlinks to the site (https://mofpi.gov.in/website-policies). The current text link to mofpi.gov.in has been removed from the ecosystem strip for that reason.
- MoFPI's identity uses the State Emblem of India. Under the State Emblem of India (Prohibition of Improper Use) Act, 2005 (https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2038), using the emblem in a way that suggests a government connection, or for any trade or business, needs prior Central Government permission; penalties include imprisonment up to two years. The 2007 Rules allow private bodies to use it only for joint events or publications with a ministry, with prior approval.
- Recommendation: keep MoFPI as text-only institutional context. If APEX later has a formal MoFPI relationship, request written permission, then a placement mockup can be prepared.
